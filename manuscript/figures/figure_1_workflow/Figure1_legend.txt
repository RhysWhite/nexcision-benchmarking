Figure 1. NEXCISION workflow. In a transposed NEXUS matrix, genomic sites are represented by coordinate-labelled rows and taxa by columns. The illustrative input contains five taxa (ntax=5) and five site rows (nchar=5). A user-supplied 1-based inclusive interval (18,000–19,000) removes the three matching site rows, including both boundary coordinates. The two retained rows remain unchanged in sequence, labels, and order; nchar is updated from 5 to 2 while ntax remains 5. Per-region removal counts and an optional provenance report can record the operation. The displayed example is synthetic and does not represent biological observations.
